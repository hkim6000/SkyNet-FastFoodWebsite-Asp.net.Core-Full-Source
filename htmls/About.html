Our story

Six stools and a grill

Flamejack started as a tiny burger counter in 2011. The grill is bigger now; the rules haven’t changed.

Burgers the way we like them

Rosa and Dev opened the first Flamejack with six stools, one flat-top and a charcoal grill they built themselves. The burgers were simple: fresh beef, a hot flame, a soft bun and a sauce Dev still won’t write down.
